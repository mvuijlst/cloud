72 new seals in the manner of the Goetia. None of them is one of the 72.
Built from a measured reading of the 1904 plates: six construction grammars in the sheet's proportions, parts and frequencies fitted to 60 hand encodings. Each seal is checked against all 80 drawings on the sheet and discarded if it is closer to one of them than real seals usually are to each other. Everything runs in this page.
